# Bunny Hop Runner Game
Welcome to the Bunny Hop Runner Game! 🐰🏃‍♀️

This is a simple web-based game where you control a bunny running through an endless course, dodging obstacles and collecting points along the way. The goal is to jump over obstacles and try to beat your high score. With each level, the difficulty increases as more obstacles appear!

# Features
*Mobile Responsive*: Play the game on any device, whether it’s a phone, tablet, or desktop.

*High Score Tracking*: The game keeps track of your high score and allows you to beat your previous best!

*Sound Effects*: Enjoy fun sound effects as you play and jump over obstacles.

# How to Play
Press the space bar or click/tap on the screen to make the bunny jump.

Avoid obstacles by jumping over them.

Keep running and collecting points to achieve the highest score possible!
